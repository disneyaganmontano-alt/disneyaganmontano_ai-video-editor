"I didn't start on the timeline — I started in the content calendar."

Before I ever opened an editor, I was running social pages: planning content, writing captions, reading the analytics, and figuring out exactly where an audience loses interest and where it leans in.

That's the part most video editors skip — and it's the part I lead with. Every cut, caption timing and hook I build is still made with a strategist's question in mind: will this survive the first three seconds of the feed?

Today I work as an AI Video Editor, combining traditional post-production (Premiere Pro, Photoshop, CapCut) with an AI-generation stack — turning briefs into claymotion explainers, AI UGC ads, and cinematic brand films, often without a single frame of real footage to start from.
